Privacy policy

Nyx is a private assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With the owner’s permission, Nyx connects to the owner’s own Google accounts to read calendar events and the sender, subject and date of messages in the owner’s inboxes. It does not read message bodies, and it cannot send, change or delete anything in those accounts.

This information is kept on a private server that only the owner can reach. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Google user data is used only to show the owner their own schedule and open items.

The owner can remove Nyx’s access at any time from their Google Account settings, and stored information is deleted on request.
